What it does

Help where you're already typing

No editor to paste into and no new app to learn. Lexio works inside Mail, Messages, Slack, your browser and everywhere else you write.

Underlines as you type

Mistakes get a quiet underline in the app you're using. Click one to see the fix and why, then apply it or ignore it.

  •       Spelling
  •       Grammar
  •       Caught by the on-device model

Rewrite any selection

Select text and a small round button appears beside it. Or press ⌃⌥L. Pick Improve, Rephrase, Translate, Shorten, Friendly or Formal, see exactly what changes, and Accept.

Know how you sound

When a message might come across as blunt, harsh or demanding, a small label appears at the end of it. One click shows a softer way to say the same thing.

Select, pick, accept

One panel for every kind of rewrite

Lexio shows its suggestion right under your selection, with removed words struck through and new words highlighted, so you always know what you're accepting.

  • Revise in your own words. Ask for “make it sound more confident” and get a new version.
  • Keeps your language. Spanish stays Spanish unless you ask to translate.
  • Undo works. If you change your mind after Accept, press ⌘Z as usual.

Private by design

Your writing never leaves your Mac

Most writing assistants send what you type to their servers. Lexio doesn't have any. It uses the Mac's own spell checker and Apple's on-device model, the one behind Apple Intelligence, and does all of its work right on your computer.

On-device model

Checks and rewrites come from Apple's Foundation Models running on your Mac's chip.

No account

Nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to.

No servers

Lexio doesn't send your text, or anything else, over the internet.

Works offline

On a plane or off the grid, it works just the same.

Everywhere you write

Mail, chat, browser and the rest

Lexio works through macOS itself, so it follows you from app to app. It's been tested in:

  • Mail
  • Messages
  • Slack
  • Safari
  • Chrome
  • Notes
  • TextEdit

Some apps share less of their text with macOS. There, the shortcut and the selection button still work even when underlines can't be drawn.

Questions

Good to know

Does Lexio send my writing anywhere?

No. Every check and rewrite happens on your Mac, using the system spell checker and Apple's on-device model. Lexio has no servers and no account, and it doesn't connect to the internet.

Why does it ask for Accessibility access?

To read what you're typing in other apps and to put a fix in place when you press Accept, Lexio uses the macOS Accessibility feature. A setup window walks you through allowing it in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility.

Why isn't it on the Mac App Store?

Apps from the App Store can't use the Accessibility feature that lets Lexio work inside your other apps. Lexio will be a signed download, checked by Apple, straight from this site.

Which languages does it check?

Spelling works in the languages your Mac's spell checker knows. The deeper grammar and tone checks are tuned and tested for English and Spanish today, and translation covers many more.

Will it work on my Mac?

If your Mac has Apple silicon, runs macOS 26 or later and has Apple Intelligence turned on, yes. On first launch Lexio tells you plainly if something is missing.
